# Early Music IPA Guide

**Work in Progress, semi-actively maintained**

## TL;DR

Upload a piece of early music sheet music and get a historically informed pronunciation guide with IPA, original text, and English translation. This free, open-source AI skill was developed for Pro Musica Tulsae's 2027 Josquin des Prez concert and is available to singers, choirs, and anyone curious about performing early vocal music.
